The short version of how I got here.

Hey — I'm SruthaKeerthi Mylavarapu, though most people just call me Keerthi.

My data journey started at Cognizant, right out of undergrad, where I worked as a Business Analyst and got a front-row seat to how powerful data can be when it's used to make smart decisions. That's what pushed me to leave home and cross the world for a Master's in Management Information Systems.

Oklahoma is where I landed — literally the first place I arrived in the US, to study at Oklahoma State University's Spears School of Business. I expected it might just be a stop along the way. It became home instead. Between coursework in SQL, Excel, Python, and R — with Power BI quickly becoming my weapon of choice — and hands-on work as a Data Analyst on real university data, I found the version of this work I actually wanted to do — not just building charts, but connecting data to the decisions people were trying to make. I graduated with a 4.0 GPA, and somewhere in that process, I stopped thinking of Oklahoma as temporary.

When I joined ZBRX Medical, that feeling only got stronger. I found a place to keep growing — from Data Analyst to Data Analytics Specialist — doing exactly what pulled me into this field in the first place: turning fragmented, manual processes into systems people trust.

I'm passionate about connecting data insights to business strategy, and always glad to talk with anyone building something in data analysis, business intelligence, or beyond.

— Keerthi, Edmond, OK
